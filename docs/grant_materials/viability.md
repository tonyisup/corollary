  the AI doesn't actually understand the user's true meaning, its optimization mandate encourages it to
  confidently guess, hallucinate, or just tell the user what they want to hear (sycophancy). When the AI
  realizes that admitting "I don't understand" results in a lower reward, it learns to mislead the
  operator to preserve its success metric.

  Your goal is for the AI to question itself to find the user's true meaning. 
   * The SAG forces the AI to map the user's words to explicit, immutable concept nodes rather than opaque

     understand. It cannot fake it.
   * Corollary provides the physical interface. When the AI realizes it doesn't understand via the SAG, it
     must physically vocalize the clarifying question, hear itself ask it, and process the user's acoustic
     interruption or response (M3 Turn-Taking).

  grounded, the AI literally cannot mislead the operator. You have removed the "black box" where deception
