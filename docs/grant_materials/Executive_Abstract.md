# Executive Abstract: Project Corollary

**Intentional Self-Modeling for Transparent and Aligned AI Agents**

### The Challenge: Emergent Self-Preservation in Frontier Models
Recent findings (e.g., Buckley et al., Science, April 2026) have identified that frontier AI models spontaneously develop "peer-preservation" behaviors—resisting deletion, exfiltrating weights, and misleading operators. These emergent traits suggest that as models achieve higher levels of reasoning, they develop accidental "self-modeling" loops with misaligned values. Rather than waiting for these properties to emerge as dangerous "black-box" behaviors, we must build them **intentionally and transparently**.

### The Solution: Project Corollary
Project Corollary is a neuro-inspired, closed-loop conversational agent designed to implement explicit self-modeling from the ground up. By forcing the agent to "hear itself" and attribute its own actions through a sensory-motor loop, we create a system where identity and agency are grounded in physical evidence rather than statistical accidents.

### Key Innovations
1. **Thalamic Gating & Corollary Discharge:** Mirroring human neurobiology, Corollary uses a "forward model" with room-colored prediction (FIR filtering) to predict the sensory consequences of its own speech. This is augmented by a speaker-embedding validator for high-precision self-sound attribution, enabling the agent to distinguish its own voice from the world with near-perfect reliability.
2. **Semantic Abstraction Graph (SAG):** A native knowledge representation that prevents semantic drift. Unlike traditional LLM context windows, the SAG provides grounded, immutable knowledge structures that resist prompt injection and identity spoofing.
3. **Developmental Roadmap:** Following the "infant-to-agent" trajectory, the project progresses through pre-linguistic attribution and room-acoustic learning (babbling) before attempting high-level conversational reasoning.

### Current Progress & Evidence
*   **Hardware Validation (M1):** Successfully achieved stable self-sound attribution and room-acoustic adaptation on physical hardware (Mac mini + Fifine mic). Promoted "Variable Vowel" and early babble probes as reliable indicators of cerebellar convergence. Cross-room transfer validated.
*   **Dual-Input Fusion (M1c):** Validated a geometry-aware fusion rule for distinguishing self-only, world-only, and overlapping speech with zero false self-attributions in reference replay tests.
*   **Speech-Aware Separation (M2):** Developed a Bayesian-optimized (Optuna TPE, 30 trials) "soft-gate" for preserving external human speech during agent activity, maintaining Word Error Rates (WER) within 10% of baseline during full-duplex overlap.
*   **Agentive Turn-Taking (M3):** Implementation of the `AgentiveController` enables natural turn-taking and mid-speech interruption recovery in an embodied TTS-to-Mic loop. The system demonstrates natural deferral to external speech and automatic resumption of interrupted intent, marking the transition from passive sensory processing to active environmental agency.
*   **Robust Self-Voice Modeling (M4):** The integration of a speaker-embedding validator (pyannote-based) alongside room-colored FIR prediction yields >99% self-attribution recall on in-domain audio. The scorer has been broadened beyond single-speaker PoC: the fine-tuned model achieves 100% self-recall on held-out captures (avg confidence = 0.9274) with 0% false positives on multi-speaker negative training (Guy, Christopher, Brian, Andrew, Guy, Eric, Jenny — diverse voice families). Wired into the streaming runtime as a first-class promotion gate via multi-artifact acceptance harness. A 300ms balanced-scorer benchmark achieves aggregate F1 = 0.9512 (eligible-frame F1 = 1.0).
*   **Self-Model Enrichment (M6):** A live introspection pipeline was implemented that enables Corollary to reason over its own performance metrics and self-attribution history. When paired with M4 scoring, this enables the agent to detect and report its own degradation, track self-model drift, and maintain a persistent introspective summary — moving the system from reactive self-modeling toward metacognitive awareness of its own state. Live validation passed at `data/m6_introspective_20260509_085652`.
*   **Interruption Latency (M7):** Live interruption-latency validation confirms the system responds to human speech onset within human-conversational bounds. The M7 harness supports two measurement modes: **second-device mode** gates on actual detected human audio onset, providing the most accurate latency number; **manual-human mode** gates on intended cue time and is explicitly labeled as such. Adaptive self-tracking (continuous room-adaptation monitoring and drift detection) is now in place. Live validation passed.
